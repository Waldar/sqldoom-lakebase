    Each game gets its own Lakebase branch, where it runs as plpgsql at 35 Hz
          (single player pauses while its tab is in the background). Every pixel of your view is computed by a
          SQL view on that branch, as many frames at a time as frames in flight. Your browser speaks the Postgres wire protocol
          through this Databricks App, which logs in with OAuth.
